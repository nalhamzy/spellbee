## Full description

Make spelling practice part of your family's routine. SpellBee brings spoken words, school lists and short spelling challenges together in one ad-free app. No account is required.

BEE ADVENTURES
Explore three illustrated worlds with Bee Buddy, each with four short stops. Play all of Sunny Meadow for free. Premium includes Moonlight Garden and Rainbow Falls. Adventure progress stays on your device.

AN INCLUDED VOICE FOR EVERY BUILT-IN WORD
Bee Buddy recordings cover all 360 built-in catalog words, their definitions and example sentences. These recordings work offline and are included for everyone. Hear a word again or use slow repeat when you need more time. Adventure narration and feedback also use included recordings.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
Type an answer, tap letter tiles, or spell aloud with optional speech recognition. Choose from eight difficulty levels and built-in themed word packs. The built-in packs remain available offline.

BRING YOUR SCHOOL WORDS
Paste a spelling list from a worksheet or message, then review and edit it before practice. Add your own definitions when helpful. Your custom lists stay on your device.

REVIEW AND FIRST-TRY PROGRESS
Today's practice mixes words at your level with words due for review. Results separate first-try answers from words completed after another attempt. Per-word review dates and practice history help you decide what to revisit.

NUMBER BEE AND MATH BEE
Practice spelling numbers as words, or listen to a sum and spell its answer. Daily quests, honey and badges add small milestones along the way.

PREMIUM FOR YOUR FAMILY'S ROUTINE
The existing Premium plans include:
• Moonlight Garden and Rainbow Falls
• Unlimited parent-made word lists
• Unlimited Math Bee rounds
• Online Studio voices for custom words

Built-in catalog words use the included Bee Buddy voice, including when Studio is selected. Online custom-word voices require an internet connection; device speech is available as a fallback.

Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices and plan details appear in your store's currency before checkout. A grown-up challenge precedes checkout. Restore Purchases is available in Settings and on the Premium screen. Manage subscription renewals in your Google Play account.

PRIVACY
Lists, Adventure progress and practice history stay on your device. No account and no ads. Optional speech recognition may use your device provider's servers. Online custom-word pronunciation sends text to our voice service. Purchases are verified securely with Google Play. See our privacy policy for operational logging details.
