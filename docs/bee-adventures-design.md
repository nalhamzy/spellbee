# Bee Adventures — 1.3

## Product decision

Add a tangible paid benefit without taking away existing free learning. Sunny Meadow is a complete free story, four stops of four words each. Premium adds Moonlight Garden and Rainbow Falls. Every story uses the current practice level. The four-word selections rotate by calendar day; this is replay variety, not a claim of new story content every day.

Finishing every word (including corrected attempts and hints) completes a story stop. Skips leave it available to retry. Story completion is intentionally separate from independent recall, which still excludes tiles, hints and same-day repetitions. Stop completion persists locally. A subscription expiring during a started round does not remove the earned story reward; new paid rounds check current access.

No timer, energy currency, penalty for missing a day, advertising, child chat, or new child data collection was added. Existing honey and ranks remain the reward system. The story screens offer optional replayable narration and readable text. Grown-ups see the purchase options only after choosing a paid benefit, and checkout requires a fresh parent challenge.

The learning rationale is to make short practice inviting while preserving useful feedback. [EEF's feedback review](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/feedback) supports actionable feedback and opportunities to use it; it does not establish efficacy for this app. SpellBee remains spelling practice rather than a complete phonics curriculum. No measured learning or conversion uplift is claimed.

## Voice

Bee Buddy is a fictional, AI-generated warm adult companion. It does not imitate a real child. Core pronunciation, definitions, examples, spelling hints, feedback and authored story narration are included offline for everyone. Premium's optional online voices apply to custom or otherwise unbundled text, with device fallback on failure. See the audio generation and verification reports under `tools/audio/`.

## Original art provenance

All three assets were generated using the built-in image generation tool on September 29, 2026, inspected, and copied into the project. No existing artwork was overwritten and no third-party character reference was used.

- `assets/adventures/meadow.png`: wide landscape gouache picture-book illustration, friendly golden bee in a blue scarf, winding meadow path, four friendly flowers, honeycomb cottage, warm honey/mint/cream morning palette, full bleed, no text or UI.
- `assets/adventures/moonlight.png`: matching picture-book illustration, golden bee and friendly moth, fireflies, four hanging lanterns along a moonlit garden path, lavender flowers, indigo/lavender/mint and warm gold, welcoming rather than scary, no text or UI.
- `assets/adventures/rainbow.png`: matching picture-book illustration, golden bee in a blue scarf, four stepping stones across a turquoise stream, waterfall and rainbow, rounded leaves and wildflowers, honey/aqua/mint/pastels, no text or UI.

All prompts requested original art for ages 6–10, central subjects suitable for a phone crop, expressive friendly faces, paper texture, clear silhouettes, no letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or licensed characters. The app draws labels and progress controls as accessible Flutter UI over or beside the artwork.
